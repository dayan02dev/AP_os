What is the TIR Journey?

The core mission of the TIR program is to engage and enable startups to run rapid prototyping experiments to fast-track and identify true product-market fit. Over an initial six-month cycle, our goal is to quickly filter for and distill exceptional founders who demonstrate absolute product and technology excellence.

This is a rolling, high-rigor journey. Startups that do not demonstrate the necessary intellectual and fundamental grit will exit the cohort, making room for new innovations every six months.

Mentorship Scope & Commitment

To ensure this remains high-yield for the startups and entirely manageable for you, we have structured the commitment with a clear baseline and maximum flexibility:

  • Time Commitment: We would ideally like a baseline commitment of one day to guide a startup, though spending more time beyond that to help out is entirely optional and based on your availability.
  • No Overburdening: We will cap the allocation to a maximum of three startups per mentor.
  • Active Selection: You won't just be assigned random teams; you will be directly involved in the process of identifying and choosing the specific startups you commit to guiding.

If you have a passion for de-risking deep-tech and want to play a foundational role in building these ventures from the ground up, we would love to have your expertise on board.

Please let me know if you would be interested in exploring this partnership, and we can set up a brief time to discuss the upcoming selection process.
