About ARTPARK

ARTPARK (AI & Robotics Technology Park) is a not-for-profit innovation hub at the Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru. We back deep-tech translation — taking research in AI, robotics, novel materials, sensors and cyber-physical systems out of the laboratory and into products that work in the real world. Since 2020 we have incubated 34 companies with a cumulative valuation exceeding ₹1,300 Cr.

What the TIR programme is

TIR is our pre-company track, built for researchers and technologists who hold lab-proven, differentiated IP but have not yet started up. Over a six-month residency at ARTPARK's IISc facilities, they mature that technology from TRL 3 (lab-validated) to TRL 4 (ARTPARK-demonstrated) while finding a real market for it.

  • ₹25 lakh kickoff grant at 0% equity for the initial six months.
  • Up to ₹2 Cr follow-on through ARTPARK's Venture Incubation Programme for those who progress.
  • Full-time space and lab access at ARTPARK@IISc, alongside IISc research groups.
  • Work spans manufacturing, space, health, agriculture, defence and other critical sectors.

This is a high-rigor, rolling cohort. Teams that do not show the necessary technical and intellectual grit exit, making room for new innovations every six months.

What we are asking of you

The role has two distinct phases, and we want to be completely transparent about both before you decide.

Phase 1 — Evaluation (short, time-boxed).

  • You receive a small, curated set of applications matched to your domain — not the whole pile.
  • You review them in our online jury portal, where each application comes with the founder's full submission, an AI-generated summary and independent reviewer scores.
  • You then select the three ventures you would most like to mentor.

Phase 2 — Mentorship (the substantive commitment).

  • You mentor the startups you personally chose — you are never assigned a team you did not pick.
  • A baseline of one day per month per startup; more is entirely optional and up to you.
  • Capped at three startups per mentor, so the commitment stays predictable.
  • Guidance is scientific, technical and commercial — helping founders de-risk the technology and find product-market fit.

Monthly honorarium

This is a paid engagement. Mentors on the panel receive a monthly honorarium for the duration of the mentorship, in recognition of the time and expertise involved. If you accept, the response form will ask for the bank details we need to process that payment. Those details are stored securely, used only for disbursement, and are never shared outside ARTPARK's finance process.

If you would prefer to contribute without an honorarium, you can say so on the form and simply skip the bank section.
