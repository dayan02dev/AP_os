What happens next

Your Founder Portal is now open. Sign in exactly as you did while applying — same email, same password. Nothing about your login has changed, and we will never ask you to reset it by email.

Your first task is to sign the MOU. You'll find it under Onboarding → Sign MOU. It sets out the terms of the residency and records your acknowledgement of each residency condition individually. Until it is signed, the cohort tabs stay locked.

Once the MOU is signed, these unlock:

  • Cohort management — Approach, Organization, and Expense management.
  • Dashboard reporting — your process dashboard, which is how we track progress together.

Available to you from day one, MOU or not: Founders resources — Art Infra (procurement), ArtConnect (fundraising and introductions), ArtPartners, Art Assets (book campus facilities), and Art Support.

Worth reading before you sign

The MOU asks you to acknowledge each of the following separately. None of them should be a surprise, but please read them properly rather than clicking through:

  • TIR is a full-time programme requiring your presence at the ARTPARK campus, unless agreed before application.
  • ARTPARK holds a first right of refusal to invest at incubation / first investment.
  • The residency runs as an expense account managed with our procurement team — funds are not paid out to company or related-party accounts.
  • Funding needed beyond the initial {{ grant_display }} to complete the residency attracts equity allocation to ARTPARK at the incubation stage.
