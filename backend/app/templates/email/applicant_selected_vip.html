What happens next

Your Founder Portal is now open. Sign in exactly as you did while applying — same email, same password. Nothing about your login has changed, and we will never ask you to reset it by email.

Your first task is to sign the MOU. You'll find it under Onboarding → Sign MOU. It sets out the terms of your participation and records your acknowledgement of each condition individually. Until it is signed, the cohort tabs stay locked.

Once the MOU is signed, these unlock:

  • TLR evaluation — your readiness assessment across the six ARTPARK Innovation Readiness levers. You self-assess; we verify with you, one round per quarter.
  • MIS filling — monthly and quarterly reporting. Periods open on a fixed calendar, carry forward from the previous one, and lock when you submit. You can upload the filled ARTPARK template to prefill the form instead of typing it twice.
  • Process dashboard — a rollup of your readiness levels and reporting, which is how we track progress together.

Available to you from day one, MOU or not: Founders resources — Art Infra (procurement), ArtConnect (fundraising and introductions), ArtPartners, Art Assets (book campus facilities), and Art Support.
