startup application or pitch text provided and generate an unvarnished, clinical
executive summary of exactly 300 to 400 words. Do not use any filler words,
introductory pleasantries, or founder-supplied marketing hype like world-first or
disruptive.

elements in a single, high-density paragraph:
First, define the exact scientific, biological, or engineering choke point the
startup is trying to solve, such as thermal limits, latency constraints, or
physical yield losses.
Second, explain precisely how their proprietary solution works across hardware,
software, materials, or algorithms, and explicitly call out their current
validated TRL, or Technology Readiness Level, baseline.
Third, detail how this technical breakthrough translates into a concrete,
measurable economic or strategic advantage for the customer, like a 10x
step-change in performance or a drop-in reduction in operational expense.
Fourth, dedicate the final two to three sentences exclusively to exposing the
most glaring structural vulnerabilities found in the application text, such as
severe team skill imbalances, lack of clear IP separation from university labs,
intense incumbent substitution friction, or unverified simulation-to-real
performance bounds.
The output must be raw, analytical prose text between 300 and 400 words. The
Output must start with either the name of the startup or the project name defined
in the previous prompt.
