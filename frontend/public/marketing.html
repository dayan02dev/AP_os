Who should apply

Built for researchers moving from lab to the real world.

We're looking for

  • Deep-tech researchers/scientists translating academic or lab IP (TRL 3+) into scalable products
  • Systems/domain technologists with experience building complex physical-digital systems
  • Teams of up to 3 founders with complementary technical, domain, and commercial skills
  • Work in AI, robotics, novel materials, sensors, mechatronics, manufacturing, or cyber-physical systems
  • Applications to manufacturing, space, health, agriculture, defense, or similar critical sectors
  • Grit for hard technical problems and a genuine passion for real-world deployment

Not a fit if

  • Your technology is below TRL 3 (not yet lab/in-house validated)
  • Your innovation is incremental rather than step-change
  • You cannot get an NOC from your current organization for full-time participation
  • You can't commit to being full-time, on-campus at ARTPARK
  • Your work is outside deep-research/advanced tech (e.g. pure SaaS or consumer apps)

FAQ

Common questions.

What's the minimum TRL I need to apply?
Your key USP IP or technology should be at TRL 3 or above — in-house or laboratory validated. Over the residency you'll mature it to TRL 4 (demonstration/validation at ARTPARK facilities) alongside identification of product-market-fit, potential customers, and use cases.
How is the funding and equity structured?
You begin with ₹25L for 6 months at 0% equity. Thereafter, ARTPARK allocates further tranches up to ₹1 Cr/yr ceiling — taking 1% equity per ₹50L allocated, plus 1% for ARTPARK space. The equity terms are valid over 12 months from kickoff. All disbursal is milestone-linked via quarterly reviews.
Who owns the IP we create?
Prior IP you bring into the project belongs to the original owners. Any new IP generated during the TIR belongs to ARTPARK — but will be exclusively licensed or transferred to you if you create a company to commercialize it. Detailed terms are discussed separately with shortlisted teams.
Can I apply as a team?
Yes, teams of up to 3 founding members are encouraged — ideally with complementary technical, domain, and commercial skills. The application asks you to add co-founder profiles.
What if I'm affiliated with another institution?
You must commit full-time to the TIR. If you're affiliated with a university, lab, or company, you'll need to secure a No-Objection Certificate (or equivalent) from that organization before joining.
Can I collaborate with other institutions?
Yes — strongly encouraged. Collaboration with academia, industrial research labs, government research labs, or industry partners is welcomed, especially where complementary IP accelerates the project.
What happens after the 12 months?
High-performing TIRs progress into ARTPARK's incubation program with follow-on equity investment pathways for the spin-out. In exceptional cases — high difficulty, high potential — the residency itself may be extended by up to one more year.
How do I submit my application?
Only via the online form linked on this page. No email, social-media, or third-party submissions are accepted. Applications must be complete at the time of submission — ARTPARK reserves the right to close the review cycle early if the cohort fills.
